Our industry has its challenges and stresses, but there are some rewarding “Oh, How Good It Feels!” moments that make it feel worthwhile.

  1. When something you built gets used for a cool purpose you didn’t conceive.

  2. When you conceive something that hasn’t been built before.

  3. Deleting code.

  4. Finding a great mentor.

  5. Having someone you mentor say “that was super helpful”.

  6. When you read code you wrote years ago and realize it’s actually not that bad.

  7. Letting your phone battery run out the evening you come off an on-call shift.

  8. When you make a mistake and realize your team has your back.

  9. When you realize you deeply understand something that was pure magic to you a couple years ago.

  10. When something you used to think you understood is now pure magic to you, and you’re deeply curious about it.
