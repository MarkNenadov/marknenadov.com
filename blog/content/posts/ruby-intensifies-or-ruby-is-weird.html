P. G. Wodehouse was known as the performing flea of British literature. But what if I tried my hand at being the performing flea of the Ruby language? I mean, aging sardonic Kotlinists can ironically experiment with other stacks, right?

I haven’t posted there in a while, but some time ago I started a substack project.

Ruby “Gems” is/was something of a museum (or is it shrine?) to the weirdness of Ruby. I figure I might as well share an index of the posts here. I think some of the are alright, all of them are byte sized.
